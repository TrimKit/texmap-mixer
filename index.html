How TexMap Mixer works

This tool packs separate images into one texture map (like roughness, metalness and AO in R, G and B), or splits a packed map back into single channels.

  1. Add your images. Click + Add images or just drop files anywhere on the page.
  2. To pack: click + New output, then pick an image and one of its channels for each of the output's R, G, B and A.
  3. To split: click Split into 4 outputs under an image and you're done.
  4. Download. Save a single output, or grab everything with Download all (ZIP).

Everything runs in your browser, so your images are never uploaded.
